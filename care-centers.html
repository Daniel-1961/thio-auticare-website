Autism Care Centers

A directory of centers and initiatives supporting families across Ethiopia.

Joy Center for Autism (Nia Foundation)

  • Name: Joy Center for Autism (operated by Nia Foundation)
  • Location: Lideta Subcity, Woreda 04, House No. 887, Addis Ababa, Ethiopia
  • Overview: Founded in May 2002 by Zemi Yenus, the Joy Center was the first specialized facility in Ethiopia dedicated to holistic rehabilitation, early intervention, life-skills training, and advocacy for children and youth with Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD).

Nehemiah Autism Center

  • Name: Nehemiah Autism Center
  • Location: Gulele Subcity / Nifas Silk-Lafto area, Addis Ababa, Ethiopia
  • Overview: Established in June 2011 by a group of families with autistic children, Nehemiah provides day-care rehabilitation, specialized training, and social integration support for children living with autism.

Bright Autism Center Hawassa

  • Name: Bright Autism Center Hawassa
  • Location: Hawassa, Sidama Region, Ethiopia (Address Code: 2FQW+5WX, Hawassa)
  • Overview: Bright Autism Center Hawassa is a specialized therapy and early intervention facility serving children with Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) in the Sidama region. The center focuses on early intervention services, parent-mediated therapy, educational psychology assessments, neurodevelopmental therapy, and neuro-biofeedback to support cognitive and social integration.

Fana-Ethiopia Autism Support (Hawassa Branch / Partner Programs)

  • Name: Fana-Ethiopia (Autism Support and Resources)
  • Location: Hawassa / Regional Operational Network, Sidama Region, Ethiopia
  • Overview: Fana-Ethiopia provides neurodiversity support, early intervention resources, and community advocacy across Southern Ethiopia. They work with families on the ground in Hawassa to deliver speech therapy resources, behavioral support (such as ABA principles), occupational therapy, and parent training initiatives aimed at celebrating neurodivergent strengths.

ECDD Inclusive Health & Education Services (Wolaita Sodo Hub)

  • Name: Ethiopian Center for Disability and Development (ECDD) – Sodo Inclusive Project
  • Location: Wolaita Sodo, Ethiopia
  • Overview: Through initiatives like the Every Life Matters (ELM) program, ECDD works in Wolaita Sodo to train local healthcare providers, improve access to inclusive health services, and support families caring for children with intellectual and developmental disabilities. While not an autism-exclusive center, ECDD serves as a primary hub for disability-inclusive advocacy, resource referrals, and educational access in the zone.

Wolaita Sodo University Special Needs Support

  • Name: WSU Special Needs & Inclusive Education Resource Center
  • Location: Wolaita Sodo University Main Campus, Sodo, Ethiopia
  • Overview: WSU operates resource frameworks and community outreach units focused on special needs education and rehabilitation awareness across the Wolaita zone, assisting local families with neurodevelopmental evaluations and referrals to regional medical centers in Hawassa or Addis Ababa.

Community-Led Programs & Local Initiatives (God's Gift Summer School)

  • Name: God's Gift Summer School / Community Inclusion Initiatives
  • Location: Wolaita Sodo, Central Ethiopia Regional State
  • Overview: Independent community and student-led initiatives provide crucial early education and skill development for neurodivergent youth and children with intellectual disabilities in Sodo.
